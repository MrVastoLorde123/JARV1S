# M45.1 — Reasoning + Uncertainty Substrate

## Decision

Introduce a provider-neutral reasoning contract between M44 `CurrentContext` and later initiative/proposal layers.

## Boundary

M45 may represent hypotheses, bounded confidence, supporting/contradicting context facts, and unresolved uncertainty.

M45 does **not**:

- establish truth;
- convert confidence into certainty;
- infer user intent as authorization;
- grant authority or permissions;
- request or perform execution;
- select a model/provider/tool;
- mutate memory or the world model.

## Lineage

Every cited fact in a reasoning hypothesis must come from the exact M44 `CurrentContext` supplied to `build_reasoning_result`. Excluded M44 facts are therefore unavailable to downstream reasoning by construction.

## Next boundary

The next layer may consume reasoning output to form initiative candidates, while preserving the separation between reasoning, evaluation, proposal, authorization, and execution.
