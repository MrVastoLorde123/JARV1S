# M28.7 — Landscape Geography and Persistent JARVIS Presence

## Decision
JARVIS should not be represented by one fixed interface layout. The environment is a family of navigable landscapes, with the current landscape acting like a place in JARVIS rather than a page in an application.

The first landscape is `OPERATIONS`, a Star-Chart-like spatial map of missions, context, runtime, abilities, active work, and recent history.

## Persistent presence
JARVIS has a recognizable persistent presence inside the world. The presence is allowed to move between regions as work changes so the operator can observe where JARVIS is currently spending attention.

The intended relationship is both:

- JARVIS can take the operator somewhere by moving toward a meaningful region;
- JARVIS and the operator can occupy the same region while activity changes the environment around them.

## Landscape model
Future landscapes may represent different dimensions of JARVIS, including:

- Operations — missions, world context, active procedures;
- Mind — memory, learning, reasoning, beliefs and context;
- Agents — persistent and temporary workers, delegation and handoffs;
- Models — LLMs, providers, model lanes and selection;
- Capabilities — skills, tools, permissions and integrations;
- Work — projects, workspaces, artifacts and execution history;
- Market — trading and external operational systems.

These are environments, not required top-level routes.

## History and reorganization
The world retains meaningful history so the operator can perceive continuity. JARVIS may also reorganize, compress, or deliberately clean the spatial representation as context changes. History should survive as meaning rather than as permanent clutter.

## Spatial constraints
Important regions receive dedicated territory. Context, JARVIS presence, detailed inspection, conversation input, and historical artifacts must not compete for the same physical area. Overlap is treated as a design defect unless intentional.

## Authority boundary
Landscape selection and JARVIS presence are presentation constructs. They do not create authority, permissions, or execution capability. Backend state and deterministic boundaries remain authoritative.
