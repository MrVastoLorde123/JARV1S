# Decision 0201 — V2 HOME Awareness and MIND Core

**Status:** LOCKED IN M27.7 / AWAITING IMPLEMENTATION

## Purpose

HOME and MIND are deliberately separate systems with different purposes.

## HOME — Awareness and orientation

HOME is the human orientation layer. It answers what matters now, what changed, what JARVIS is focused on, what is active, what needs the user's presence, and where the user should look next.

HOME may surface information derived from MIND, Projects, Plans, Capabilities, Devices, Models, and runtime state, but it does not become MIND or replace those systems.

HOME also provides a system overview including device specifications and model/provider assignments as they become available.

## MIND — Core internal world

MIND is the dedicated core environment for JARVIS's internal intellectual state. It is expected to become substantially more advanced than a navigation tree or graph widget.

MIND may expose memory, context, thinking, plans, learning, knowledge, evidence, relationships, timelines, and other future cognitive structures through explorer, graph, and contextual views.

The explorer answers where something lives. The relationship graph answers what it is connected to. The core contextual surface answers what it means within the current JARVIS state.

## Architectural rule

HOME may summarize and orient. MIND may represent and organize the internal world. HOME must not absorb MIND's core purpose, and MIND must not be reduced to a landing page.

The interface remains a representation and interaction boundary. Visibility never grants authority.
