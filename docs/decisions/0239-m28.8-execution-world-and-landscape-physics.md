# M28.8 — Execution World and Landscape Physics

## Decision
JARVIS World is a living representation of JARVIS's work, not a collection of application pages. The interface must expose meaningful layers of execution while preserving the user's ability to remain oriented.

## Contract
- JARVIS is an abstract intelligence entity with mechanical characteristics; his visible form can change with the work being performed.
- JARVIS movement must have a distinct visual language based on flow through the environment, not generic page transitions or cursor movement.
- Agents are persistent world entities during active work. A worker may be created by JARVIS, leave his vicinity, enter another landscape, select a model, execute work, and return with a result.
- Execution visibility is intentional: planning, delegation, discovery, execution, assimilation, and return should be spatially legible.
- Technical users may expand Agent Logs to inspect literal execution steps without forcing the detailed log into the primary world view.
- Discovery is represented as throughput plus structure: a sieve/funnel communicates filtering and a growing node graph communicates conceptual connections.
- Key discoveries should eventually resolve to traceable evidence and citations; the prototype establishes the visual slot for that future contract.
- Landscapes are different environments, not renamed copies of one layout.
- MIND behaves as a knowledge constellation; AGENTS as a moving worker network; MODELS as an intelligence/provider constellation; CAPABILITIES as a structured tool/permission lattice; WORK as project/workspace terrain; MARKET as an external signal/trading field; OPERATIONS as the navigation/command world.
- World history remains available, but the environment may reorganize around relevance: active work occupies the foreground, important knowledge remains near, old research drifts outward, finished work can become archival structure, and dead agents can disappear.
- Returning after absence should produce a compact asynchronous state briefing that answers what changed, what matters now, and what happens next.

## Boundaries
These visuals express state and provenance; they do not grant authority, certainty, permission, or truth. The backend remains authoritative.

## Implementation
M28.8 adds the shared execution-world layer, landscape-specific visual physics, cross-landscape travel events, expandable logs, throughput and cognitive-structure visuals, and an expanded return-state briefing.
