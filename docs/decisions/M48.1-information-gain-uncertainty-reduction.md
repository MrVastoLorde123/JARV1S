# M48.1 — Information-Gain Boundary Decision

M48 separates uncertainty reduction from initiative evaluation.

Evaluation may identify descriptive value, feasibility, and urgency. M48 may then represent what additional information could reduce named unresolved uncertainty and how much information gain is expected.

This layer remains descriptive. An information-gain opportunity is not a scheduled task, notification, authorization request, provider selection, tool selection, or execution request. Uncertainty remains unresolved until independently reduced by later evidence and verification.
