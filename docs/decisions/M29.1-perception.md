# M29.1 — Perception

## Decision
JARVIS gains a provider-neutral perception contract over the existing environment-observation architecture. Perception describes filesystem, process, network, service, log, and hardware state; it does not grant authority or execute actions.

## Contract
- Every observation has explicit source, environment, domain, availability, timestamp, and provenance.
- Observation payloads are immutable at the contract boundary.
- Snapshots reject mixed environments and duplicate observation identities.
- Missing or unavailable sensors remain observations with explicit availability rather than fabricated values.
- Freshness remains a separate concern and may use the existing environment freshness services.
- Perception contains explicit `authority_granted=False` and `execution_requested=False` defaults.
