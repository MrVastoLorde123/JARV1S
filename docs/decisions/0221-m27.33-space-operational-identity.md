# Decision 0221 — M27.33 Space Operational Identity

## Decision
The major interface spaces share one JARVIS visual language but express different jobs:

- **CHAT** is the intent surface: open, directional and composition-led.
- **CONTROL** is the execution and authority surface: structured lanes and stronger boundaries.
- **MIND** is the context and relationship surface: quieter, connective and exploratory.
- **CAPABILITIES** is the usable power inventory: denser and more actionable.

These are not independent applications. They are different operational views over the same JARVIS state.

## Constraints

- Preserve shared mission/runtime state rather than duplicating state per space.
- Prefer additive CSS/controllers over rewriting the App shell.
- Use real contracts for visual state; do not fabricate cognition, agents or runtime activity.
- Light and dark themes must express the same structure using appropriate materials.

## Result

M27.33 establishes a distinct visual grammar for CHAT, CONTROL, MIND and CAPABILITIES and makes the environment feel like one operating system with specialized surfaces rather than four styled pages.
